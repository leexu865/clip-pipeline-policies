Privacy Policy

Clip Pipeline

Last updated September 11, 2026

1What this is

Clip Pipeline is a personal, single-operator content tool. One individual runs it locally on their own computer to generate short-form videos and publish them to social accounts they own. It is not offered as a service to the public, does not have a user base beyond its operator, and does not collect data from anyone other than the account it is authorized to post to.

2What we access, and why

When you connect a platform (TikTok, YouTube) to Clip Pipeline, you grant it the following, and nothing more:

user.info.basicReads your open ID, display name, and avatar — used only to confirm which account is connected. Never displayed, shared, or stored beyond the local session.
video.uploadUploads a video file you generated as a draft to your own connected account. Clip Pipeline never reads, modifies, or deletes any of your existing content.

3Where your data lives

Authentication tokens are cached in a file on the operator's own machine (tiktok_token.json, youtube_token.json). They are never transmitted to any third-party server, never sold, and never used for advertising or analytics. Nothing here talks to a hosted backend — the entire pipeline runs locally.

4Third parties

Clip Pipeline does not share data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party. The only outside services it talks to are the platform APIs (TikTok, YouTube) needed to publish content, and an AI provider used to generate script text and narration audio — neither receives your account credentials.

5Revoking access

You can revoke Clip Pipeline's access at any time from your TikTok or YouTube account's connected-apps settings. Doing so immediately invalidates the cached token; deleting the local token file has the same effect.
